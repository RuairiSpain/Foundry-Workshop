# Cascadia Outfitters store manual (excerpt)

## Store hours

| Location | Weekdays | Weekends |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Seattle flagship | 9am–8pm | 10am–6pm |
| Portland | 10am–7pm | 10am–6pm |

## Price matching

Store associates can match a competitor's listed price on an identical,
in-stock item from another authorized retailer. Marketplace listings
(eBay, Facebook Marketplace) don't qualify.

## Curbside pickup

Orders placed online for in-store pickup are held for 5 days. After 5
days, the order is restocked and the customer is refunded automatically.

## Warranty claims at the register

Associates can process a worn-gear guarantee claim (see return policy)
directly at the register for items under $200. Claims at or above $200
route to the online returns process instead.
