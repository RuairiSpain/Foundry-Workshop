# Cascadia Outfitters return policy

## Standard returns

Return unused gear within 60 days of delivery for a full refund. Items
must have original tags attached and show no signs of use.

## Worn-gear guarantee

Footwear and apparel with defects, including issues found after normal
use, are covered for one year from the purchase date. This applies even
if the item shows wear — the guarantee is against defects, not against
use.

## Non-returnable items

- Climbing protection (cams, nuts, carabiners) once removed from
  packaging, for safety reasons.
- Food and fuel canisters.
- Gift cards.

## How to start a return

1. Sign in to your account and open **Order history**.
2. Select the order and choose **Start a return**.
3. Print the prepaid return label. Returns without a Cascadia label are
   not eligible for free return shipping.

## Refund timing

Refunds post within 5 business days of the warehouse receiving the
item. Store-credit refunds post immediately at any branch register.
